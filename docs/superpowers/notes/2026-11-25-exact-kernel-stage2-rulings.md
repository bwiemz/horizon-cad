# Exact kernel, stage 2: the controller's rulings

Stage 2 was built task by task, with a fresh implementer and a fresh reviewer for each task. Where the plan or spec left a question open, or a review found the plan wrong, the controller ruled on it, and kept going rather than waiting for an answer. Each ruling below states what was decided, why, and what it costs if it is wrong. They are the decisions taken on the owner's behalf, kept verbatim from the build ledger, in the order they were made. Later rulings amend earlier ones where they say so.
