# Horizon CAD User Guide

Horizon CAD is a CAD application for 2D drafting and 3D parametric modelling.
You can draw in 2D, build parts from sketches and features, put parts together
in assemblies, and make drawing sheets of them. Files open and save in
Horizon CAD's own formats, and in STEP, DXF, STL, glTF, PDF and SVG.

Press **F1** (on macOS, **Cmd+?**) at any time to open this guide at the
page for what you are working on.

## Contents

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): the window, moving around the view,
  and a first part in five steps.
- [2D drafting](drafting.md): drawing and changing lines, circles, arcs and
  polylines; snaps and typed coordinates; dimensions, text, hatches, blocks
  and layers.
- [Sketches and constraints](sketches.md): sketches on planes and faces,
  geometric and dimensional constraints, variables and configurations.
- [Parts](parts.md): extrude, revolve, holes, fillets, shells, patterns and
  the other features, and the feature tree.
- [Assemblies](assemblies.md): components, mates, exploded views, patterns,
  interference and the bill of materials.
- [Drawing sheets](drawings.md): views, sections and details, dimensions and
  the title block.
- [Files](files.md): the kinds of document, opening and saving, importing
  and exporting, autosave and recovery, and crash reports.
- [Settings](settings.md): preferences, units and languages.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md).
